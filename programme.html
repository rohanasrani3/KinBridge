Parenting Foundations

Four weeks. Four skills.

One 90-minute live session a week — at a partner centre or on Zoom — plus a short Home Lab to try with your child.

Understand your style

Week 2

Encourage the good

Home Lab: three rules and a praise log.

Build a safe home

Home Lab: let them own one decision.
Week 4

Talk and repair

Listen, stay calm, reconnect after conflict.
