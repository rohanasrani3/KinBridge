Why it matters

Family pressure is the top reason young people reach out for help.

Study pressure, long working hours and habits handed down from our own parents feed each other. Few parents ever get practical help with it.

The gap

More funding than ever, but still hard to find help that fits.

Parent-education spending rose 166% in four years, to HK$170M. The support is free and credible — but broad, optional, and rarely measured.
